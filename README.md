### Jiayun Jin

Statistician and survey methodologist (PhD, KU Leuven), based in the Netherlands.
My research is on measurement and data quality in surveys and other self-report data:
how response processes such as midpoint and extreme responding shape observed answers,
how response quality can be monitored during data collection, and how to adjust for
selection in non-probability samples.

**Research code**

- [spcsurvey](https://github.com/jinjiayun57/spcsurvey): R package with nonparametric multivariate control charts for monitoring survey response quality
- [disentangling_gender_attitudes](https://github.com/jinjiayun57/disentangling_gender_attitudes): IRTree and latent profile analysis of response processes in attitude items (*Sociological Methods & Research*, 2026)
- [nonprob-survey-inference](https://github.com/jinjiayun57/nonprob-survey-inference): simulations comparing calibration, inverse probability weighting and doubly robust estimation
- [liss-panel-quality](https://github.com/jinjiayun57/liss-panel-quality): LISS panel composition compared with CBS benchmarks, and panel attrition

**Teaching**

- [Data Analysis with R](https://jinjiayun57.github.io/data-analysis-with-r/): course site for a university-wide course I designed and taught at Renmin University of China (2024, 2025)
- [ml_modeling-foundations](https://github.com/jinjiayun57/ml_modeling-foundations): notebooks on core machine-learning methods in Python
